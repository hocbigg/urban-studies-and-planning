## Conduct your own site analysis

Document and interpret the physical layout, natural systems, architectural character, and historical development layers of a specific parcel, street corner, or urban block.

- [Assignments Overview: Reading the City (MIT OpenCourseWare, Anne Whiston Spirn)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-016j-the-once-and-future-city-spring-2015/pages/assignments/) - A four-stage project workflow with guidelines for investigating site boundaries, natural processes, historical morphology, and physical traces of change.
- [Assignment 1: Select A Site (MIT OpenCourseWare, Anne Whiston Spirn)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-016j-the-once-and-future-city-spring-2015/pages/assignments/assignment-1-select-a-site/) - Step-by-step guidance on establishing observational boundaries, framing research questions, and recording baseline site conditions.
- [Planning Developments: Site and Context Analysis Guide (Welsh Government / Design Commission for Wales)](https://www.gov.wales/planning-developments-site-and-context-analysis-guide) - A comprehensive professional guide outlining how to evaluate microclimate, movement infrastructure, landscape features, and built-form scale.

## Design your own street cross-section

Reallocate existing roadway right-of-way among travel lanes, dedicated transit corridors, protected bike facilities, green infrastructure, and pedestrian sidewalks.

- [Streetmix](https://streetmix.net/) - An interactive, open-source web application for modeling, resizing, and illustrating complete street cross-sections to scale.
- [Urban Street Design Guide (National Association of City Transportation Officials)](https://nacto.org/publication/urban-street-design-guide/) - The primary open-access technical manual for dimensioning vehicular lanes, pedestrian bulb-outs, transit priority lanes, and stormwater curb extensions.

## Build your own transit accessibility map

Model transit network performance and spatial coverage to identify service deserts and evaluate how effectively public transportation connects residents to employment.

- [Assess Access to Public Transit (Esri Learn ArcGIS)](https://learn.arcgis.com/en/projects/assess-access-to-public-transit/) - A hands-on tutorial calculating walking service areas around transit stops and overlaying demographic census data to locate underserved communities.
- [Transit Network Analysis Tools (GitHub, Esri)](https://github.com/Esri/public-transit-tools) - An open-source toolkit and documentation for building scheduled transit networks from General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) data to analyze travel-time accessibility.

## Publish your own interactive planning storymap

Translate technical urban plans, spatial datasets, and policy recommendations into an engaging, multimedia narrative format for public consultation.

- [Storify a Planning Report (Esri Learn ArcGIS)](https://learn.arcgis.com/en/projects/storify-a-planning-report/) - A step-by-step project transforming a municipal neighborhood plan into an interactive digital story using maps, 3D scenes, and community feedback mechanisms.

## Write your own policy brief

Draft a concise, evidence-based document that translates technical urban research into concrete, actionable policy options for municipal decision-makers.

- [Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/policy-briefs/) - A structured academic guide explaining the rhetorical structure, framing, audience tailoring, and formatting requirements of professional policy briefs.
- [How to Write a Policy Brief (International Development Research Centre)](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding/resources-idrc-grantees/how-write-policy-brief) - Practical methodology on structuring executive summaries, policy alternatives, and research-backed recommendations for public officials.
- [Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Policy-Brief-Guide.pdf) - A practical walkthrough detailing visual hierarchy, editing timelines, and plain-language translation for policy documents.

## Compile your own community needs assessment

Collect, synthesize, and analyze localized demographic, economic, and physical conditions to identify systemic service gaps and establish neighborhood priorities.

- [Assessing Community Needs and Resources (Community Tool Box, University of Kansas)](https://ctb.ku.edu/en/table-of-contents/assessment/assessing-community-needs-and-resources) - A step-by-step toolkit outlining survey design, asset mapping, focus groups, and secondary data synthesis for local communities.
- Housing Needs Assessment Tool (Local Housing Solutions / NYU Furman Center) - Search 'Housing Needs Assessment' on Local Housing Solutions for their framework on compiling local housing affordability metrics, rent burdens, and supply deficits.

## Conduct your own walkability audit

Execute a field-based safety and accessibility evaluation of a specific pedestrian corridor, documenting infrastructure deficiencies and micro-scale design barriers.

- [AARP Walk Audit Tool Kit (AARP Livable Communities)](https://www.aarp.org/livable-communities/getting-around/aarp-walk-audit-tool-kit.html) - A comprehensive toolkit featuring downloadable checklists and worksheets for assessing sidewalk continuousness, crossing safety, lighting, and driver behavior.
- [All About Walk Audits (America Walks)](https://americawalks.org/all-about-walk-audits/) - Methodological guidance on scouting routes, recruiting diverse audit teams, engaging local stakeholders, and converting field notes into advocacy campaigns.
- [How to Conduct a Walk Audit in Your Community (America Walks)](https://americawalks.org/how-to-conduct-a-walk-audit-in-your-community-quick-video-guide-for-assessing-your-neighborhood-walkability/) - A quick reference guide outlining ten procedural rules for executing safe and productive street audits.

## Record your own community oral history

Gather, record, and archive first-person qualitative testimonies from local residents to document the social memory and lived experience of neighborhood change.

- [The Smithsonian Folklife and Oral History Interviewing Guide (Smithsonian Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage)](https://folklife.si.edu/the-smithsonian-folklife-and-oral-history-interviewing-guide/smithsonian) - A manual providing sample release forms, open-ended question guides, audio recording advice, and interview preservation protocols.
- [How to Do Oral History (Smithsonian Institution Archives)](https://siarchives.si.edu/history/how-do-oral-history) - Methodological steps for interviewee recruitment, pre-interview research, ethical obligations, and transcript processing.
- [Principles and Best Practices for Oral History (Oral History Association)](https://oralhistory.org/principles-and-best-practices-revised-2018/) - The established professional ethical and procedural standard for planning, conducting, archiving, and indexing oral narratives.

## Build your own exploratory scenario plan

Develop a set of plausible alternative futures driven by critical uncertainties to test how community policies perform under unpredictable conditions.

- [How to Use Exploratory Scenario Planning (XSP): Navigating an Uncertain Future (Lincoln Institute of Land Policy)](https://www.lincolninst.edu/publications/policy-focus-reports/how-use-exploratory-scenario-planning-xsp) - A detailed manual with workshop agendas, matrix templates, and case studies for identifying driving forces, constructing scenario axes, and testing interventions.
- [Scenario Planning KnowledgeBase (American Planning Association)](https://www.planning.org/knowledgebase/scenarioplanning/) - A curated collection of practitioner guides, tool evaluations, and municipal case studies on integrating scenario analysis into master plans.

## Draft your own climate action plan

Formulate an integrated municipal framework establishing greenhouse gas emissions baselines, sectoral reduction targets, and local climate hazard adaptation measures.

- [Introducing the Climate Action Planning Guide for Cities (C40 Knowledge Hub)](https://www.c40knowledgehub.org/s/article/Introducing-the-climate-action-planning-guide-for-cities) - A modular step-by-step roadmap for scoping emission sources, developing climate vulnerability assessments, and prioritizing municipal mitigation actions.
- [Climate Action Planning Framework (C40 Knowledge Hub)](https://www.c40knowledgehub.org/s/article/Climate-Action-Planning-Framework) - The core technical standard defining baseline requirements, governance structures, and monitoring metrics for local climate resilience plans.
