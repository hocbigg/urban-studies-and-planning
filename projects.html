Conduct your own site analysis

Document and interpret the physical layout, natural systems, architectural character, and historical development layers of a specific parcel, street corner, or urban block.

  • Assignments Overview: Reading the City (MIT OpenCourseWare, Anne Whiston Spirn) - A four-stage project workflow with guidelines for investigating site boundaries, natural processes, historical morphology, and physical traces of change.
  • Assignment 1: Select A Site (MIT OpenCourseWare, Anne Whiston Spirn) - Step-by-step guidance on establishing observational boundaries, framing research questions, and recording baseline site conditions.
  • Planning Developments: Site and Context Analysis Guide (Welsh Government / Design Commission for Wales) - A comprehensive professional guide outlining how to evaluate microclimate, movement infrastructure, landscape features, and built-form scale.

Design your own street cross-section

Reallocate existing roadway right-of-way among travel lanes, dedicated transit corridors, protected bike facilities, green infrastructure, and pedestrian sidewalks.

  • Streetmix - An interactive, open-source web application for modeling, resizing, and illustrating complete street cross-sections to scale.
  • Urban Street Design Guide (National Association of City Transportation Officials) - The primary open-access technical manual for dimensioning vehicular lanes, pedestrian bulb-outs, transit priority lanes, and stormwater curb extensions.

Build your own transit accessibility map

Model transit network performance and spatial coverage to identify service deserts and evaluate how effectively public transportation connects residents to employment.

  • Assess Access to Public Transit (Esri Learn ArcGIS) - A hands-on tutorial calculating walking service areas around transit stops and overlaying demographic census data to locate underserved communities.
  • Transit Network Analysis Tools (GitHub, Esri) - An open-source toolkit and documentation for building scheduled transit networks from General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) data to analyze travel-time accessibility.

Publish your own interactive planning storymap

Translate technical urban plans, spatial datasets, and policy recommendations into an engaging, multimedia narrative format for public consultation.

  • Storify a Planning Report (Esri Learn ArcGIS) - A step-by-step project transforming a municipal neighborhood plan into an interactive digital story using maps, 3D scenes, and community feedback mechanisms.

Write your own policy brief

Draft a concise, evidence-based document that translates technical urban research into concrete, actionable policy options for municipal decision-makers.

  • Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center) - A structured academic guide explaining the rhetorical structure, framing, audience tailoring, and formatting requirements of professional policy briefs.
  • How to Write a Policy Brief (International Development Research Centre) - Practical methodology on structuring executive summaries, policy alternatives, and research-backed recommendations for public officials.
  • Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab) - A practical walkthrough detailing visual hierarchy, editing timelines, and plain-language translation for policy documents.

Compile your own community needs assessment

Collect, synthesize, and analyze localized demographic, economic, and physical conditions to identify systemic service gaps and establish neighborhood priorities.

  • Assessing Community Needs and Resources (Community Tool Box, University of Kansas) - A step-by-step toolkit outlining survey design, asset mapping, focus groups, and secondary data synthesis for local communities.
  • Housing Needs Assessment Tool (Local Housing Solutions / NYU Furman Center) - Search 'Housing Needs Assessment' on Local Housing Solutions for their framework on compiling local housing affordability metrics, rent burdens, and supply deficits.

Conduct your own walkability audit

Execute a field-based safety and accessibility evaluation of a specific pedestrian corridor, documenting infrastructure deficiencies and micro-scale design barriers.

  • AARP Walk Audit Tool Kit (AARP Livable Communities) - A comprehensive toolkit featuring downloadable checklists and worksheets for assessing sidewalk continuousness, crossing safety, lighting, and driver behavior.
  • All About Walk Audits (America Walks) - Methodological guidance on scouting routes, recruiting diverse audit teams, engaging local stakeholders, and converting field notes into advocacy campaigns.
  • How to Conduct a Walk Audit in Your Community (America Walks) - A quick reference guide outlining ten procedural rules for executing safe and productive street audits.

Record your own community oral history

Gather, record, and archive first-person qualitative testimonies from local residents to document the social memory and lived experience of neighborhood change.

  • The Smithsonian Folklife and Oral History Interviewing Guide (Smithsonian Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage) - A manual providing sample release forms, open-ended question guides, audio recording advice, and interview preservation protocols.
  • How to Do Oral History (Smithsonian Institution Archives) - Methodological steps for interviewee recruitment, pre-interview research, ethical obligations, and transcript processing.
  • Principles and Best Practices for Oral History (Oral History Association) - The established professional ethical and procedural standard for planning, conducting, archiving, and indexing oral narratives.

Build your own exploratory scenario plan

Develop a set of plausible alternative futures driven by critical uncertainties to test how community policies perform under unpredictable conditions.

  • How to Use Exploratory Scenario Planning (XSP): Navigating an Uncertain Future (Lincoln Institute of Land Policy) - A detailed manual with workshop agendas, matrix templates, and case studies for identifying driving forces, constructing scenario axes, and testing interventions.
  • Scenario Planning KnowledgeBase (American Planning Association) - A curated collection of practitioner guides, tool evaluations, and municipal case studies on integrating scenario analysis into master plans.

Draft your own climate action plan

Formulate an integrated municipal framework establishing greenhouse gas emissions baselines, sectoral reduction targets, and local climate hazard adaptation measures.

  • Introducing the Climate Action Planning Guide for Cities (C40 Knowledge Hub) - A modular step-by-step roadmap for scoping emission sources, developing climate vulnerability assessments, and prioritizing municipal mitigation actions.
  • Climate Action Planning Framework (C40 Knowledge Hub) - The core technical standard defining baseline requirements, governance structures, and monitoring metrics for local climate resilience plans.
